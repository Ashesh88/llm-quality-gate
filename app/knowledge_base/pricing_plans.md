# Subscription Tiers and Pricing Guide

## Tier Overview
1. **Starter Plan ($29/month or $290/year)**
   - Includes 5 active team seats.
   - 10,000 monthly automation workflow executions.
   - Standard email support (response within 24 business hours).
   - Rate limit: 60 requests per minute on API endpoints.

2. **Professional Plan ($99/month or $990/year)**
   - Includes 25 active team seats.
   - 100,000 monthly automation workflow executions.
   - Priority email and Slack community support (response within 4 business hours).
   - Webhooks, custom CRM sync (HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira).
   - Rate limit: 300 requests per minute.

3. **Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing - starts at $499/month)**
   - Unlimited seats and workflow executions.
   - Dedicated Customer Success Manager + 99.9% uptime SLA.
   - 24/7 Phone, WhatsApp, and PagerDuty emergency escalation.
   - Self-hosted on-premise deployment option available.
