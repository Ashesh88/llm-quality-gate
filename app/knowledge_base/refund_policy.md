# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
All new customers on the Starter ($29/month) and Professional ($99/month) plans are eligible for a 100% full refund within 14 calendar days of their initial purchase, no questions asked.

## 2. Refund Eligibility & Exclusions
- **Eligible:** Initial monthly or annual subscriptions requested within 14 days of transaction.
- **Non-refundable:** Enterprise plans with custom SLA agreements, one-time onboarding setup fees, and usage-based overage charges (e.g. AI token overages, extra SMS units).
- **Pro-rated Refunds:** For cancellations after 14 days, service remains active until the end of the billing period; no partial or pro-rated cash refunds are issued unless an outage caused by our infrastructure exceeded SLA commitments.

## 3. How Refunds are Processed
- Refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI) within 5-7 business days after approval.
- An automated support ticket must be generated with category `BILLING_REFUND` to trigger payment gateway reconciliation.
